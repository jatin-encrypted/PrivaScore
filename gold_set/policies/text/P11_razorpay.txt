2. Information we collect and how we use it
We collect and store your Personal Information. If you provide your third-party account credentials ("Third Party Account Information") to us, you understand that, upon your authorization, certain content and information from those accounts may be transmitted to your Razorpay account. Any Third Party Account Information transmitted to us will be covered by this Privacy Policy. You may choose not to provide certain information; however, this may prevent you from registering with us or using some of our features and services.
We will limit the collection of Personal Information to what is necessary to fulfill the purposes identified below. If we use or plan to use your information for a purpose different from the one for which it was collected, we will request your consent beforehand.
The Personal Information we collect will be used solely to enable you to use the services provided by us, promote a safe and secure experience, assess user interest in our products and services, inform you about online offers and updates, troubleshoot issues, customize your experience, detect and prevent errors, fraud, and other criminal activity, process payments, enforce our terms and conditions, and fulfill any other purpose disclosed to you at the time of collection.

3. Account information of Merchants
If you create an account to take advantage of the full range of services offered on Razorpay, we ask for and record Personal Information such as your name, email address, and mobile number. We may collect and store your Sensitive Personal Data or Information—such as financial information, including credit card details, debit card details, bank account information, and Know Your Customer ("KYC") documents as required by RBI regulations—as well as any other applicable information that you choose to save in your Razorpay account.
We use your email address to send you updates, news, and newsletters (if you willingly subscribe during or after signup), and to contact you on behalf of other users for example, when someone sends you a friend request, personal message, or participates in a social collaboration event. If you do not wish to receive communications unrelated to your use of our services, you can unsubscribe by clicking the link provided at the bottom of any email from us. We also use your mobile number to send you transaction-related SMS alerts based on your preferences. If you do not wish to receive such messages from us, please notify us at razorpay.com/support to stop receiving SMS messages.
Razorpay assures you that your Personal Information will not be disclosed publicly or sold to any third party.
You have the option to erase any information you have provided, including your Personal Information. If you choose to erase your data, Razorpay will delete all your stored information from its servers.

Section 4(a) - Use of Razorpay Services Through External AI Assistants (MCP Connectors)
Razorpay provides optional integrations that allow Users to access certain Razorpay services through third-party AI assistants and LLM platforms (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude etc) (‘AI Integrations’). These integrations are completely optional and are activated solely at your discretion.
When you choose to use an AI Integration, the third-party AI platform may send certain instructions, queries, or data that you choose to submit through that platform to Razorpay’s MCP server in order to fulfill your request. Razorpay processes such data only for the purpose of executing the requested operation, monitoring and debugging.
Razorpay does not share any User or Merchant information with an AI platform except for data requested by Users or Merchants. By choosing to enable or interact with an AI Integration, you acknowledge that the content you submit to the external AI platform may be processed by that platform according to its policies before being transmitted to Razorpay.
